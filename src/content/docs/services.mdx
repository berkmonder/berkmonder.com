I work with companies that want AI to do real work: reliably, measurably, and at a cost they
understand. I can build it with you, set up what it runs on, and teach your team to own it.

<Section label="LLM & agent systems" id="llm">

Assistants that answer from your own data and agents that use your tools, with evaluation and
tracing from day one. On cloud APIs or models you host yourself.

- Retrieval-augmented assistants over your documents and databases
- Agents that call your internal tools and APIs, with guardrails
- Evaluation sets, tracing and feedback, so quality is measured, not guessed
- Choosing between cloud APIs and self-hosted models: privacy, latency, cost

**A good fit if** you have a promising prototype that isn't reliable enough to ship, or you want to
start the right way.

</Section>

<Section label="ML infrastructure & MLOps" id="mlops">

Pipelines, experiment tracking, CI/CD for models, serving and monitoring, so models keep working
after launch, at a cost you can see.

- Data and training pipelines (Airflow, Dataform, Prefect) on GCP or your own servers
- Experiment tracking and a model registry with MLflow
- CI/CD that turns every model into a versioned, reproducible artifact
- Serving, monitoring and alerting; GPU and cloud costs under control

**A good fit if** your models work in notebooks, but getting them to production is manual, fragile
or expensive.

</Section>

<Section label="Training for your team" id="training">

Hands-on workshops on your own use cases, half a day to several days, remote or on-site in
Istanbul, in Turkish or English. Your team keeps the material.

- LLMs and NLP for engineers: embeddings, retrieval, evaluation
- Building agents: tools, memory and safety, from scratch to production
- Practical AI for every team: using assistants well and safely
- MLOps fundamentals for data and ML teams

**A good fit if** you want your people to use and build with AI confidently.

</Section>

<Section label="How it works">

<Steps>

1. **Intro call.** Thirty minutes, free. You tell me where you are and what you need; I tell you
   honestly whether I can help.

2. **Proposal.** A short written plan: scope, deliverables, timeline and a fixed price.

3. **Build or teach.** Small steps with regular check-ins, so you see progress early and can steer.

4. **Handover.** Documentation and a walkthrough, so your team can run and change it without me.

</Steps>

</Section>
