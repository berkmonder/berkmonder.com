# Privacy Policy
**Last updated:** 2026-04-19

This Privacy Policy explains how **YAP - Your Adaptive Planner** handles user information.

## 1. Data Collection
We do **not collect, store, or share any personal data**.

All data created within the app (such as planner entries) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third parties.

## 2. Local Data Storage
The app stores your data only on your device. We do not have access to this data, and it is not backed up to external servers unless you choose to use your device's own backup features.

## 3. Third-Party Services
This app does not use any third-party services that collect user data.

## 4. Data Security
Since your data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's security measures.

## 5. Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any users.

## 6. Future Changes
We may introduce new features in the future that involve data collection or external services. If that happens, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
